We carpool out of the city to trade pavement for wooded paths, fresh air, and good company, with a bagel or pastry stop on the way home. Our Rockefeller State Park run is a gentle, scenic loop where all paces are welcome. Our Wildcard run explores a new spot each month for runners ready for a bit more adventure. Whether you've never run on trails before or you're a seasoned trail runner, you're in the right place.
